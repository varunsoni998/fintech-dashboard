CustomHolidays – Grab Price (Chrome extension)
==============================================

WHAT IT DOES
1) AUTOMATIC (main use): in Price Compare click "⚡ Get exact prices" (on a hotel card, or at the bottom for all
   your hotels). The extension opens each Booking.com / Expedia / Hotels.com / MakeMyTrip deal link in a
   background tab of your Chrome, reads the final total for the stay, closes the tab and fills it in as
   "✓ Confirmed on site (auto)". You don't open anything. Takes ~10–30 seconds. Keep Chrome open.
   (Agoda's total only appears at checkout, so Agoda keeps Google's price – it has matched exactly in tests.)
   If a site can't be read, that row says "Couldn't … — use View deal".

2) MANUAL (any site): on a hotel BOOKING / CHECKOUT page (Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia, Hotels.com, MakeMyTrip or the hotel's own
website), click the extension → it reads the final total → "Send to Price Compare" → in BusinessOS click
"Apply as confirmed". The price becomes ✓ Confirmed (used for ranking and the Excel). No typing.

It only opens the deal links Price Compare gives it, and only when you click "Get exact prices" (or reads the page
you're on when you click the ₹ icon). It never logs in, types anything into a site, or books anything.

INSTALL (once per computer, ~2 minutes)
1. Unzip "grab-price-extension.zip" into a folder you will keep (e.g. Documents\grab-price-extension).
   Don't delete or move that folder afterwards – Chrome loads the extension from it.
2. Open Chrome and go to:  chrome://extensions
3. Turn ON "Developer mode" (top-right switch).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped "grab-price-extension" folder.
5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and PIN "CustomHolidays – Grab Price" (₹ icon).
6. Open BusinessOS → Price Compare and refresh. You should see "✓ Grab-price extension" at the top.

HOW TO USE
1. In Price Compare, Search the city and open the hotel (See all prices / View deal).
2. On the website, choose the room and go to the BOOKING step where the final total is shown
   (Agoda: "Price", Expedia: "Total", Booking.com: "Total price" / "Price"). Don't use the search-list price.
3. Click the ₹ icon → check the amount it found (pick another line or type it if needed) → "Send to Price Compare".
4. Price Compare shows a green "Price grabbed" box with the hotel already picked → "Apply as confirmed".

TIPS
- Make sure the dates, rooms and guests on the website match the trip in Price Compare.
- For 2+ rooms, grab the total for ALL rooms.
- If the ₹ total isn't found, type it in the box – it still saves you copying it across.

UPDATING
When a new version is sent: replace the folder's files, then on chrome://extensions click the ↻ (reload) icon
on the extension.
